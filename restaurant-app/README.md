# Restaurant App

A Letterboxd-style restaurant discovery and logging app for food-obsessed locals and out-of-towners, starting in St. Louis, MO. Curated and opinionated rather than exhaustive — social trust (friends' ratings) drives discovery instead of algorithmic ranking or ad placement.
