Prep Time:

5 mins

Total Time:

5 mins

Servings:

20

Yield:

1 1/4 cups

### Why You'll Love This Recipe

- Use this homemade Creole seasoning to boost the taste of rice, meats, soups, and more.
- Reviewers praise the familiar flavor, similar to famous Louisiana seasoning blends.
- "I made this for a roast turkey I did for my co-workers." —Allrecipes member Cameron Tucker

## Ingredients

Original recipe (1X) yields 20 servings

- 5 tablespoons paprika
- 3 tablespoons salt
- 2 tablespoons onion powder
- 2 tablespoons garlic powder
- 2 tablespoons dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons dried basil
- 1 tablespoon dried thyme
- 1 tablespoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon white pepper
- 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper

## Directions

1. Gather all ingredients.
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
2. Combine paprika, salt, onion powder, garlic powder, oregano, basil, thyme, black pepper, white pepper, and cayenne pepper.

3. Store in an airtight container.
