Prep Time:

45 mins

Cook Time:

2 hrs 30 mins

Total Time:

3 hrs 15 mins

Servings:

10

### Why You’ll Love This Recipe

- Create authentic Cajun tradition at home with this gumbo featuring a rich, dark roux.
- Home cooks appreciate the simple, tomato-free ingredient list for authentic Louisiana taste.
- Allrecipes member Ashley confirms, "...this is the only recipe I've ever found that is truly spot on."

## Ingredients

Original recipe (1X) yields 10 servings

- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 pound andouille or smoked sausage, sliced ¼-inch thick
- 2 celery ribs, chopped
- 1 large green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 pinch Creole seasoning, or to taste
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 rotisserie chicken, boned and shredded

## Directions

1. Gather all ingredients.
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
2. Heat oil in a stockpot over medium heat; whisk in flour until roux is smooth and has cooked to the color of chocolate milk, about 8 to 10 minutes. Be careful not to burn the roux. If you see black specks in the mixture, it's best to start over.
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
3. Add sausage, celery, bell pepper, and onion into the roux; cook and stir 5 minutes. Add garlic; continue to cook and stir another 5 minutes.

   ![A hand pouring a small bowl of minced garlic into a pot of roux with sausage, celery, bell peppers, and onions](<https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/3ep4CU3apbdbdUhmmfAncfqA4AM=/750x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/AR-141114-Cajun-Chicken-and-Sausage-Gumbo-ddmfs-4x3-step-02-557fd6dd5bea4f019d78ec9ed4e9b599.jpg>)
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

4. Season with salt, pepper, and Creole seasoning; stir well to blend. Pour in chicken broth and add bay leaf. Bring to a boil over high heat; reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
5. Stir in the chicken and simmer 1 hour more. Skim off any foam that floats to the top during this time.
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
6. Serve and enjoy!

   ![A bowl of Cajun chicken and sausage gumbo served over rice, garnished with sliced green onions](<https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/xQHh44eGEdkCEwELC95LstnsN9M=/750x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/AR-141114-Cajun-Chicken-and-Sausage-Gumbo-ddmfs-4x3-beauty-0da2b06cb8e143d3919463aeb6b3495a.jpg>)
   Dotdash Meredith Food Studios

### Cook’s Note

If you prefer a darker roux or more intense flavor, keep stirring until it's the color you desire.
