Ready 45 min

Servings

## INGREDIENTS

- 1 Tablespoon [black bean](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/black-bean) sauce
- 2 Teaspoon [honey](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/honey)
- 1 Teaspoon [hoisin sauce](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/hoisin-sauce)
- 1 Teaspoon Ground [Sichuan peppercorns](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/sichuan-peppercorns)
- 1 Teaspoon Ground [Cumin](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/cumin)
- 1 Teaspoon [Rice wine vinegar](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/rice-wine-vinegar)
- Pinch of [Garlic](https://www.bigoven.com/article/details/garlic) powder

## INSTRUCTIONS

Mix all ingredients in a small bowl.
