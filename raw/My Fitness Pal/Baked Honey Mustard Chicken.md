This juicy Baked Honey Mustard Chicken from [The Honour System](https://thehonoursystem.com/) is easy to throw together and pops right into the oven. Just mix up your marinade, pour over the chicken and bake. Dinner’s ready in an hour with minimal effort.

**Baked Honey Mustard Chicken**

**Ingredients**

- 24 ounces uncooked Boneless, Skinless Chicken Breasts
- ¼ cup Honey (local if possible)
- ¼ cup Mustard (preferably stone ground)
- 1 tablespoon Parsley, fresh chopped
- 1 tablespoon Dried Basil
- 1 teaspoon Smoked Paprika
- 1 tablespoon fresh Lemon Juice
- Sea Salt & Pepper, to taste

**Directions**

1. Preheat oven to 375 Fahrenheit.
2. Rinse and pat dry chicken breasts and place in a large glass dish. Season them with sea salt and pepper.
3. In a small bowl, stir together the remaining ingredients and pour over the chicken. Flip each one over a few times to coat it well.
4. Cook for 45-50 minutes, turning and basting them half way through.

Note: Make sure the chicken breasts are fully cooked by inserting a meat thermometer into the thickest part of the breast to assure an internal temperature of at least 165 degrees Fahrenheit. If you don’t have a meat thermometer, slice into the thickest part of the breast to assure there is no pink.
